Birds of a Feather Help: Context-aware Client Selection for Efficient Federated Learning

Hangrui Cao, Yifei Zhu, et al.

International Workshop on Federated Learning, AAAI 2022 Oral

A novel neural combinatorial contextual bandit (NCCB) intelligently selects clients in each federated round while preserving privacy. The method surpasses the state-of-the-art Oort in both convergence speed and final accuracy.

Large-scale Data Analysis and Group Work Pattern Recognition with the GitHub Archive Dataset

Hangrui Cao, advised by Qiaozhu Mei

Working paper · University of Michigan

A large-scale empirical study of how sentimental emoji and text usage relate to GitHub contributors' behavior. Methods include GNN-based social graph embedding, LINE, regression, clustering, and NLP processing.

Hangrui Cao, Jing Wu, et al.

Resolving the “Shaky Ground Truth” Problem in Driver Behavior Classification

Hangrui Cao, advised by Carol Flannagan

University of Michigan Transportation Research Institute

Lightweight multi-class CNN for driver behavior classification (90.2% accuracy); designed loss functions, confident learning, and Bayesian CNNs to model aleatoric uncertainty (0.0162) when labels themselves are uncertain.
